## 1. Overview

The Apple of Eden is a bio-engineered, shelf-stable, single-serving fruit designed to provide 100% of the daily nutritional requirements for an average adult human (male/female, ages 18-50). One fruit per person per day. No preparation required. No refrigeration required. Natural preservative skin allows indefinite shelf life under normal conditions.

**Taste Profile:** A unique, novel sweet fruit flavor not found in nature. No cultural or nostalgic associations. Universally palatable across all human populations.

**Form Factor:** Apple-shaped, semi-translucent, hard skin, soft juice interior. Bite skin, suck juice, discard skin (biodegradable).

## 4. Production Method

The Apple of Eden is produced using an adaptation of the OpenSourceTerracore Food Synthesizer (Mk2).

**Step 1: Base Mycelium Cultivation**

- Oyster mushroom mycelium (Pleurotus ostreatus) grown on sterilized grain substrate
- Incubation period: 10-14 days
- Result: High-protein fungal biomass

**Step 2: Nutritional Fortification**

- Mycelium biomass is blended with pre-formulated Nutrition Cartridge
- Cartridge contains: Complete vitamin/mineral mix, essential fatty acids, plant-based calcium/iron, B12 fortification
- Cartridge is open-source (recipe published separately)

**Step 3: Flavor & Color Cartridge**

- Unique sweet fruit flavor (proprietary blend, published open-source)
- Natural golden-amber coloring (carotenoid-based)

**Step 4: Printing & Setting**

- Mixture is printed into apple-shaped mold
- Surface exposed to controlled dehydration to form hard skin
- Interior remains semi-liquid (juice consistency)
- Setting time: 5-10 minutes at 40-50°C

**Step 5: Quality Control**

- Weight check (250-300g)
- Skin integrity check (no cracks)
- Nutritional spot check (random sampling)

## 5. Shelf Life & Storage

| Condition | Shelf Life |
|-----------|------------|
| Room temperature (20-25°C) | Indefinite (5+ years) |
| Refrigerated (4°C) | Indefinite |
| Frozen | Indefinite |
| High humidity (>80%) | 6-12 months (skin may soften) |
| Direct sunlight (UV) | 12-24 months (nutrient degradation) |

**Preservation Mechanism:** The hard skin acts as a natural moisture barrier and antimicrobial layer. Composition is similar to honey-derived preservatives (low water activity, acidic pH, natural enzymes). No artificial preservatives required.

## 6. Consumption Instructions

1. Bite through the skin at any point.
2. Suck out the soft juice interior.
3. Discard skin (biodegradable – compostable).
4. One fruit provides complete 24-hour nutrition for an average adult.

**Note:** Not recommended for children under 5, pregnant women, or individuals with specific medical conditions without medical supervision. Pediatric and specialized formulas to be developed separately.
